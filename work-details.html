OpenGovtBD

Project Overview

🌐 OpenGovtBD is a full-stack Government Citizen Engagement Platform designed to bridge the gap between citizens and government services in Bangladesh 🇧🇩. The goal is to make government information more accessible, organized, and user-friendly — so that people can find the services they need without friction.

The platform focuses on a clean, responsive interface, structured access to public services, and a foundation that can grow into a wider civic-tech tool. It is an ongoing learning project where I apply modern full-stack development practices end to end.
